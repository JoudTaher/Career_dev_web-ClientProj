Learn with a client

Client-based courses

Students apply information skills to real organizational needs while practicing teamwork, client communication, project management, and professional delivery.

SI 350

UX Field Research in the Public Sector

Student teams conduct user research around an information need identified with a government or nonprofit partner.

SI 485/495

Information Analytics Capstone

Advanced undergraduate teams scope a client problem and develop data-oriented analysis, tools, and recommendations.

SI 487/497

User Experience Capstone

Students use UX research and design methods to address a client challenge across the capstone sequence.

SI 500

Problem-Solving with People, Information, and Technology

Student teams frame an information problem and develop a practical response with an external organization.

SI 538

Citizen Interaction Design

Students collaborate with public-sector partners to improve how communities access information and services.

SI 547

Engaging with Communities

Students build reciprocal community relationships while responding to information needs and shared goals.

Learn beyond a course

ELO programs and community engagement

Program availability and eligibility can change. Confirm current details through the official UMSI program page.

AI Clinic

Small collaborative teams help organizations identify and implement appropriate AI tools. The official listing includes current U-M student eligibility and timing.

SO-ELL

Student Organization Engaged Learning Leaders complete a semester-long information project for a community-based organization.

Alternative Spring Break

UMSI students support nonprofit, cultural, government, or educational organizations through focused capacity-building projects.

Alternative Fall Break

UMSI students contribute UX or data analytics skills to Detroit small businesses and nonprofit organizations.

A2 Data Dive

An annual data-for-good event where students and community members contribute at different skill levels.

UMSI Service Day

A one-day opportunity for UMSI students, faculty, and staff to participate in local service projects.

Other ways to apply your learning

Global, research, entrepreneurship, and applied learning

The same preparation, collaboration, ethical engagement, and reflection practices can support many kinds of real-world learning.

Global experiences

Prepare for cultural context, local knowledge, access needs, and ethical partnership before working across communities or countries.

Research

Clarify the research purpose, roles, data practices, consent expectations, supervision, and how findings will be shared.

Entrepreneurship

Define the problem, test assumptions with people affected by it, document decisions, and communicate the value of the work.
