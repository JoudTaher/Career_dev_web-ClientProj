Stage 1 of 3

Preparing for your experience

A little preparation helps you enter a project with purpose, realistic expectations, and respect for the people involved.

At this stage

Build a strong foundation

  • Identify what you want to learn and contribute.
  • Understand the course, program, and partner expectations.
  • Learn about the community or organization before making assumptions.
  • Consider the skills, access needs, and support you may need.

Three actions

What to do before you begin

Set learning goals

Write two or three specific goals that connect the experience to your coursework, professional interests, or personal growth.

Try this: “By the end of this experience, I want to be able to…”

Know the context

Review information about the partner, community, or research setting. Notice what you still need to ask instead of filling gaps with assumptions.

Try this: List what you know, what you think you know, and what you need to learn.

Plan for participation

Check dates, transportation, technology, accessibility, safety, and communication requirements early enough to request support.

Try this: Add key deadlines and meetings to one calendar.
