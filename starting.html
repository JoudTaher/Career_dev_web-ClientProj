Stage 2 of 3

Starting your experience

Use the beginning of the project to build trust, make responsibilities visible, and agree on how the group will work together.

At this stage

Create shared expectations

  • Learn how each person prefers to communicate and participate.
  • Clarify the project purpose, boundaries, and intended outcomes.
  • Assign roles while keeping responsibility shared across the team.
  • Agree on meeting routines, response times, and decision-making.

Three actions

Set the team up for success

Begin with relationships

Make time for introductions and listen for the knowledge, goals, and constraints each person brings to the work.

Ask: “What would make this collaboration valuable for you?”

Define the project together

Turn a broad challenge into a shared project statement. Confirm what is in scope, what is not, and who approves major changes.

Document: purpose, audience, deliverables, milestones, and open questions.

Create a communication plan

Choose tools and routines that work for the full team and partner. Avoid spreading important decisions across too many channels.

Agree on: meeting cadence, primary channel, response time, and note-taking.

Starter tools

Create these shared documents

  • Team agreement

    Record roles, working preferences, accessibility needs, decision rules, and how the team will address missed commitments.

    Worksheet idea

  • Project plan

    List the major activities, owners, dependencies, milestones, and review dates in one place everyone can access.

    Planning tool

  • Partner communication log

    Keep concise meeting notes, decisions, questions, and follow-up responsibilities visible to the team.

    Tracking tool

Pause and reflect

Whose expectations are still unclear?

Identify one assumption your team is making. Turn it into a respectful question for the person who can clarify it.
