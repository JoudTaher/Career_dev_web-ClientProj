Stage 3 of 3

During your experience

Keep the project healthy by communicating consistently, making progress visible, and responding to challenges with curiosity and care.

At this stage

Make learning and progress visible

  • Review the project plan and adjust it openly when conditions change.
  • Share progress and questions with the partner before deadlines.
  • Give specific feedback about the work, not the person.
  • Reflect regularly instead of waiting until the experience ends.

Three practices

Keep the collaboration moving

Communicate before there is a problem

Use brief, regular updates to show what is complete, what comes next, and where the team needs input or support.

Update format: progress, next step, question, risk.

Navigate conflict constructively

Name the shared goal, describe the specific issue, listen for different needs, and agree on one next action. Ask an instructor or ELO staff member for support when needed.

Begin with: “I noticed… The impact is… Can we talk about…?”

Practice ethical engagement

Protect confidential information, respect community knowledge, question who benefits from decisions, and avoid promising work the team cannot deliver.

Check: Who is affected, who has a voice, and who makes the final decision?

Use when needed

Quick project supports

  • Weekly progress check

    Compare completed work with the plan, identify one risk, and decide who owns each next action.

    5-minute checklist

  • Feedback conversation guide

    Describe the situation, explain its effect, ask for the other perspective, and agree on a concrete change.

    How-to guide

  • Midpoint reflection

    Record what you are learning, what is challenging your assumptions, and what you will try differently next.

    Reflection activity

Pause and reflect

What changed because you listened?

Identify one moment when teammate or partner feedback changed your approach. What did that teach you about collaboration?
