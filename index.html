What is engaged learning?

Engaged learning connects classroom knowledge with real people, organizations, communities, and challenges. Your experience might involve a client-based course, community partnership, research project, global experience, or entrepreneurial idea.

The UMSI Engaged Learning Office supports opportunities that are collaborative, experiential, and reflective. This resource hub helps you focus on the actions that matter at each stage.
